How this list is made

The list has 281 confirmed films. A film is included only when a source says its Kannada version was released in theatres or on a streaming service.

Where the films come from

Most entries come from Wikipedia. A script reads every film article that uses Wikipedia's film infobox and keeps those that say the film was dubbed into, or released in, Kannada. Others come from news reports of theatrical and streaming releases, and from the language listings on Sacnilk. Films first made in Kannada, or shot in Kannada alongside another language, aren't included.

How entries are checked

Each film shows the sentence that puts it on the list and links to where that sentence comes from. When a source only says a Kannada version was planned or announced, the film goes under "Announced, not yet confirmed" until another source confirms it. Duplicate spellings of the same film are merged, keeping every source.

How often it updates

A scheduled job checks Wikipedia and streaming news every week. New Wikipedia matches are added automatically; news reports are checked by hand before they're added.

Dubbing into Kannada

For decades the Kannada film industry kept an informal ban on dubbed films. In 2017 the Competition Commission of India ruled that dubbed films can't be blocked, and Fast & Furious 8 was released in Kannada as one of the first in over four decades. Since 2020, streaming services have added Kannada audio to many Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Hindi and Hollywood films.

Spotted a mistake? Report it here.

Every film is listed with the source that shows its Kannada version. This site lists where films were released. It doesn't host or link to copies of films.
